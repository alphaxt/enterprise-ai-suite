ENTERPRISE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT & SLA
Document ID: MSA-2026-CLOUD-994
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Parties: CloudMatrix Solutions Inc. ("Provider") and Global Logistics Corp ("Customer")

SECTION 1: SERVICE AVAILABILITY & UPTIME COMMITMENT
1.1 Service Level Guarantee: Provider warrants that the Enterprise AI Cloud Platform shall maintain an aggregate Monthly Uptime Percentage of not less than 99.95% during each billing calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
1.2 Scheduled Maintenance: Routine platform upgrades shall occur strictly between Sunday 01:00 UTC and 04:00 UTC, with at least 72 hours prior written notice to Customer via the administrative alert dashboard.
1.3 Service Credit Penalties:
    - Availability between 99.50% and 99.94%: 10% monthly subscription credit.
    - Availability between 99.00% and 99.49%: 25% monthly subscription credit.
    - Availability below 99.00%: 50% monthly subscription credit and right to immediate contract termination without penalty.

SECTION 2: DATA PRIVACY, CONFIDENTIALITY & ZERO-RETENTION
2.1 Ownership: All Customer Data, proprietary prompts, uploaded documents, vector indices, and fine-tuned artifacts remain the exclusive intellectual property of Customer.
2.2 Zero Model Training: Provider explicitly warrants and certifies that Customer Data shall never be logged, cached, or utilized to train, retrain, or improve foundational commercial LLM models without explicit written bilateral addendum.
2.3 Encryption Standards: Data in transit must be protected using TLS 1.3 with AES-256-GCM cipher suites. Data at rest across all vector partitions, object stores, and relational caches must be encrypted using customer-managed KMS keys (FIPS 140-3 Level 3 compliance).

SECTION 3: LIABILITY CAPS & INDEMNIFICATION
3.1 Limitation of Liability: Except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breaches of Data Privacy under Section 2, total aggregate liability of either party under this agreement shall be capped at 200% of fees paid by Customer in the preceding 12 months.
3.2 Intellectual Property Indemnity: Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer against any third-party claims, lawsuits, or damages alleging that Provider's AI inference system infringes any copyright, patent, or proprietary trade secret.

SECTION 4: TERMINATION & DATA OFFBOARDING
4.1 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate with 60 days advance written notice following completion of the initial 12-month Minimum Commitment Period.
4.2 Post-Termination Offboarding: Within fourteen (14) calendar days following contract cessation, Provider shall execute irreversible cryptographic sanitization (DoD 5220.22-M standard) of all customer document embeddings, backups, and audit traces, and furnish a signed Certificate of Destruction.
